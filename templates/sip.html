Calculate your Systematic Investment Plan returns

About this tool

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. Instead of a large lump sum, you invest a small fixed amount every month like an EMI, but into a portfolio instead of a loan. Over time, your money grows through compounding, meaning you earn returns on your returns, not just on the original amount.

Compounding a simple example

Investing Rs. 5,000/month at 12% annual return for 20 years:
Total invested — Rs. 12 Lakhs  |  Final corpus — approx. Rs. 49.96 Lakhs  |  Wealth gained — approx. Rs. 37.96 Lakhs.
Your money did most of the heavy lifting, not you.

Key terms

Monthly SIP Amount

The fixed amount you invest each month. Even Rs. 500/month is a great start consistency matters more than size.

Duration (Years)

How long you keep investing. More years = exponentially more wealth. Patience is the investor's superpower.

Expected Annual Return (%)

Indian equity mutual funds have historically returned 10–15% per year. Fixed deposits give around 6–7%.

Final Corpus

Total value at the end your invested amount plus all returns earned over time.

Total Invested

The raw money you put in monthly, added up. e.g. Rs. 5,000 x 12 months x 10 years = Rs. 6 Lakhs.

Wealth Gained

Final Corpus minus Total Invested — pure profit from compounding, with no extra effort from you.

How to use

  1. 1 Set the Monthly SIP amount — whatever you can comfortably invest each month without straining your budget.
  2. 2 Try different durations — compare 10, 15, and 20 years to see how dramatically time affects your final corpus.
  3. 3 Adjust the return rate — use 6–7% for FD/debt, 10–12% for diversified equity funds, 14–15% for aggressive equity.
  4. 4 Watch the chart update live — it shows year-by-year corpus growth vs how much you actually invested.
  5. 5 Click "Optimize My SIP Portfolio" — to find the best asset mix to invest your SIP into.

Results are projections based on assumed constant returns. Actual returns vary with market conditions. SIP does not guarantee profit.
