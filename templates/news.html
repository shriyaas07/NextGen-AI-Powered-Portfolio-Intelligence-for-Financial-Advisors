About this tool

This page pulls live financial news from Indian media: Economic Times, Business Standard, Moneycontrol, Mint, and more all in one place. Each article is automatically tagged with a sentiment label so you can gauge the overall market mood without reading every headline.

Sentiment labels explained

Positive

Headline contains words like "rise," "gain," "rally," "record," or "surge."

Negative

Headline contains words like "fall," "crash," "decline," "weak," or "drop."

Neutral

Informational or policy news without a strong directional tone either way.

Why does news matter for investing?

Markets are partly driven by human emotions. Positive news (e.g., RBI cuts rates, a company posts record profits) makes investors buy, pushing prices up. Negative news causes them to sell. Staying informed helps you understand why markets move so you don't panic-sell or over-invest at the wrong time.

How to use

  1. 1 Articles load automatically — sorted by most recent. No action needed.
  2. 2 Check the sentiment badge — top-left of each card. Use it to scan today's overall market mood quickly.
  3. 3 Click "Read more" — to open the full article on the original source website.
  4. 4 Cross-reference with the Screener — if a specific company is mentioned, jump to the Screener to check its performance data.

Sentiment analysis is keyword-based and may not always reflect the true tone of an article. Read the full article before making any investment decisions.
