Portfolio Optimizer

Enter your investment details and get 3 optimized portfolios instantly

About this tool

This is a smart money-splitting machine. You tell it how much you have and what types of investments you're open to, and it finds the best possible mix using a Nobel Prize-winning method called Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT). It produces 3 ready-made portfolios Balanced, High Return, and Low Risk so you can pick the one that fits your goals.

Key terms

Asset Class

A category of investment stocks, gold, crypto. Mixing them reduces overall risk.

Expected Return (%)

How much your money might grow per year on average, based on historical data.

Volatility (%)

How much the portfolio value bounces up and down. Lower = smoother ride.

Sharpe Ratio

Return earned per unit of risk an efficiency score. Above 1.0 is excellent.

Balanced Portfolio

A middle-ground mix decent returns without extreme risk. Good for first-time investors.

Projected Corpus

How much your investment could grow to over your chosen time period.

How to use

  1. 1 Set your investment amount — drag the slider anywhere from Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 1 Crore.
  2. 2 Choose your time horizon — how many years do you plan to stay invested? Longer generally means better returns.
  3. 3 Select at least 2 asset classes — Equities and ETFs are on by default. Add Gold, Silver, Crypto, or Real Estate as needed.
  4. 4 Click "Optimize My Portfolio" — wait ~30 seconds while the algorithm runs 1,000+ simulations.
  5. 5 Review your 3 portfolios — compare the cards and the Efficient Frontier chart, then save the one you like.
