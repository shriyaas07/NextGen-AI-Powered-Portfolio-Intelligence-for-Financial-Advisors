Stock Screener

About this tool

A search engine for stocks. Instead of researching each company one by one, you set criteria like "show me stocks that grew at least 15% per year and aren't too risky" and it instantly filters all NIFTY 500 stocks. It also fetches live market prices and generates a Buy / Hold / Caution signal for each stock.

Key terms

Sharpe Ratio

Return earned per unit of risk a "worth-it score." Above 1 is good, above 2 is great.

Volatility (%)

How wildly the stock's price moves. High = unpredictable. Low = steady and predictable.

Annual Return (%)

Average yearly growth historically. 20% means Rs. 100 became Rs. 120 on average each year.

52W High / Low

Highest and lowest price over the last 52 weeks. Shows where the stock sits in its yearly range.

Day Change (%)

How much the price moved today vs yesterday's close. Green = up, Red = down.

Signal

Auto-generated from Sharpe, return, and day change. A quick data snapshot not financial advice.

How to use

  1. 1 Set your filters — Min Sharpe, Max Volatility, Min Annual Return. Leave as defaults to see all stocks.
  2. 2 Choose how to sort — by Sharpe for most efficient stocks, or by Annual Return for top performers.
  3. 3 Click "Apply Filters" — the table loads matching stocks and starts fetching live prices automatically.
  4. 4 Click any row — to instantly refresh that stock's live price from the exchange.
  5. 5 Use "Refresh Live Prices" — to update all visible stocks at once.

Signals are algorithm-generated for educational purposes. Always research independently before investing. Live prices may be delayed.
